🔌 How Mode B Works on Your Windows 11 PC (iPhone 16 Pro Max & 18 Pro Max)

  1. One-Time iPhone Setting (Crucial for Windows 11 USB-C): On both your 16 Pro Max and 18 Pro Max, open Settings → Photos, scroll to the bottom under "Transfer to Mac or PC", and select Keep Originals.
  2. Copy Both Phones into One Combined Folder on Your PC: Plug in Phone 1 via USB-C, open This PC → Apple iPhone → Internal Storage → DCIM, and copy the folders into a single PC directory (e.g. C:\Users\You\Pictures\Combined_iPhone_Backup). Repeat for Phone 2 into that same folder (or subfolders inside it).
  3. Review & Click "Move Selected to PC Archive": This app compares every photo across both phones, keeps the 🌟 Best Quality Shot in your main folder, and moves duplicates, blurry takes, screenshots, WhatsApp forwards,banners, and New Year/Morning wishes into PC_Archive_Moved\ with SHA-256 verification.
  4. Clean the Connected iPhones over USB-C Automatically: Every time you archive files, the app generates prune_connected_iphone.ps1 inside your Archive folder. Plug in your 16 Pro Max (and then 18 Pro Max) over USB-C and run that script—it deletes only the archived duplicates/junk from the iPhone's DCIM storage while leaving all your best photos on the iPhone!
